JUMP ATTACK KILLS
=================

Counts the mobs you kill with a jump attack, shows the count on screen, and saves it across sessions.

A kill counts when a mob near you dies while your jump attack is going on or in the 1.5 seconds after
it. A mob someone else kills near you in that time counts too.

The first time you play with it, it starts from the game's own count for its jump attack kill achievement, so kills
from before you installed it are in it.

To show or hide the counter, move it, change its size, or reset the count, open the game's Settings, Mods,
Jump Attack Kills. "Log Jump Attacks" writes how many mobs were near and how many died for each jump attack to the
mod's log file.

REQUIREMENTS
------------

- BetterBlueprintLoader, which must also be installed

INSTALL
-------

1. Close the game, and fully close the Minecraft Launcher if you use it.

2. Open your Minecraft Dungeons II Paks folder.
    Steam: Right click the game in your library, then Manage > Browse local files, then open Dungeons\Content\Paks
    Minecraft Launcher: C:\XboxGames\Minecraft Dungeons II\Content\Dungeons\Content\Paks

3. Create a folder called ~mods inside the Paks folder if there is not one already.

4. Extract this zip into ~mods, so the files end up in: Paks\~mods\JumpAttackKills

UNINSTALL
---------

Delete the JumpAttackKills folder from ~mods.
